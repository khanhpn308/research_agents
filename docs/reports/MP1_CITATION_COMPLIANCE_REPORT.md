## 3. S01 Specific Correction Audit

1. **Barsi et al. (2025):** Verified that the report describes this work strictly as a linear shear-deformable rope model providing upper/lower bending stiffness bounds based on kinematic eigenstrain limit states. It is NOT claimed to be a predictive solver for evolving pressure-controlled local stick-slip contact.
2. **Tjahjanto et al. (2017):** Verified that the report cites this work for modeling cable cores under external radial pressure via 3D FE with Coulomb contact penalty. The report explicitly emphasizes that chamber pressure $p$ does NOT equal inter-wire normal force $f_n$.
3. **Kang et al. (2020):** Verified that Kang et al. is cited for incremental shape-memory UMAT and solid FE under tensile loading, without repeating historical worker errors claiming corotational beam elements or pure-bending validation.
4. **Vahidi et al. (2021):** Verified that the constitutive formulation is accurately identified as the Souza et al. thermodynamic model coupled to frictional surface contact in Abaqus, serving as the strongest existing precedent for H0b.
5. **Xin Liu (2004):** Verified as a 2004 MSc thesis with no DOI, using CableCAD-derived flexural rigidity.
