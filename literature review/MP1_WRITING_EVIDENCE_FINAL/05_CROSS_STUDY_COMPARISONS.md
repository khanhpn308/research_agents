## Explicit non-comparabilities

- E, EI, operational K, tangent/secant slope, stiffness ratio and hysteresis area are distinct observables.
- Pressure at source, pressure near specimen and local normal traction are distinct variables.
- Stick at a contact, structural jamming, partial slip, full slip and plastic deformation are not synonyms.
- M22 and M23 share underlying cable data; M17/M18/M22/M26 include accepted pilot reuse. Do not count these as independent corroborations.

## Evidence-role rule

`DIRECT_SUPPORT` means the accepted record states the claim for the target section. `CONTEXT_ONLY` and `COMPARATOR` identify records that inform comparison but cannot be used as direct support. `METHODOLOGY` covers review organization or reporting rules. `AUTHOR_PROPOSAL` and `PENDING` are writing/research decisions, not published findings.
