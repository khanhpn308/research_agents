Keywords:                                                   In the past decades, the robotics community has made great progress in the development of soft robots that are
Soft robotics                                               inherently safe in their interaction with their environment. However, the compliance of these robots also comes
Granular jamming                                            with drawbacks, such as the fact that soft structures cannot easily exert high forces on their environment. In this
Electro-conjugate fluid (ECF)
                                                            work, we propose a new soft actuator where part of the structure can actively switch from being compliant to
ECF micropump
                                                            rigid by using particle jamming in some sections. A challenge with jamming particle designs is that it requires a
Micro hydraulic power source
                                                            vacuum pump which tends to be bulky, which we have addressed here by directly embedding a flexible electro-
                                                            conjugated fluid (ECF) micropump in the actuator. This bidirectional pump does not affect the flexibility of the
                                                            soft actuator, but when it is switched on, pressures up to ± 55 kPa are achieved. We demonstrate that these
                                                            embedded pumps can be used both to increase the stiffness of the actuator with a factor of 5.5 by an ECF pressure
                                                            of − 55 kPa as well as to inflate the actuator and achieve hydraulic actuation with a bending angle of 16◦ by + 55
                                                            kPa. We believe that the proposed soft actuator that can actively switch stiffness and produce deformation is an
                                                            important tool for the development of more practical soft robots.
