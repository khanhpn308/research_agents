# WP-B locator issues

- Printed-page integers are `NOT_REPORTED` for all new records where the PDF convention was not independently stable; physical `pdf_page` is used.
- Equation glyphs containing Greek/superscript symbols may require human typography check before thesis transcription; scientific locator and page are verified.
- M17/M18 records are reused from Pilot R1 and retain their existing locator corrections; no IDs or Pilot files were changed.
