# WP-B terminology notes

- **layer/laminar jamming:** sheet stack whose interlayer friction is modulated by compression.
- **wire/fiber jamming:** fiber or wire bundle with frictional contacts; do not merge with sheet layers.
- **positive-pressure jamming:** internal positive pressure compacts granules/fibers/layers; pressure path differs from vacuum.
- **source pressure:** pressure supplied by pump/bladder; not automatically local normal traction.
- **pre-slip/transition/full-slip:** state labels used by source models; preserve source-specific definitions.
- **equivalent stiffness:** system-level slope/force-deflection quantity under stated boundary conditions.
- **homogenized/continuum model:** model-level label; does not imply independent experimental validation.
