# M37 — Paper Note

## A. Bibliographic information
Matsumoto, Cho, Takashima, and Suzuki (2024), *Effect of R-phase on shape recovery speed of Ti-Ni shape memory alloy wire for variable-stiffness mechanism using jamming transition phenomenon*. DOI: 10.1299/mej.24-00130. Canonical PDF: `data/chap1-2_citations/M37__2024-Effect of R-phase on shape recovery speed of Ti-Ni shape memory alloy wire for variable-stiffness mechanism using jamming transition phenomenon.pdf`.

## B. Research problem
Đánh giá ảnh hưởng của R-phase đến tốc độ hồi phục Ti–Ni wire và khả năng cải thiện actuator cho variable-stiffness/jamming device. [M37-E01]

## C. Physical mechanism and system
Device kết hợp Ti–Ni element với coffee-ground jamming; vacuum giữ hình dạng, heating làm SMA hồi phục. Nghiên cứu tập trung material state và recovery, không đo ma sát interface. [M37-E01, M37-E06]

## D. Mechanical models and assumptions
Không có reduced-order bending/friction model chính; tác giả dùng σA và EA2 từ tensile curves làm descriptors của recovery velocity. [M37-E03–E05]

## E. Experimental methods
Wire 0.9 mm, heat treatment/water quench, DSC xác nhận R-phase/Af; recovery test 70 mm wire bent 90°, 0.5 V, camera/LabVIEW; tensile test gauge 30 mm ở 363 K và 5% strain; training 50 cycles, 3% strain ở 353 K. [M37-E01–E03, M37-E07]

## F. Important equations and figures
Figs. 1–2 device/prior stress; Table 1 specimen states; Figs. 3–4 recovery method; Figs. 5–10 tensile/recovery; Figs. 11–17 device and training.

## G. Quantitative findings and validation
R-phase specimens tended to have higher recovery velocity; 773 K–0.3 ks showed high σA/low EA2. Training reduced plastic strain from 0.37% to 0.03%, improved recovery capability, but velocity was approximately unchanged. [M37-E04–E08]

## H. Limitations and counterevidence
Af, manufacturer and heat treatment differ; R-phase devices initially failed full recovery; training improved shape recovery but did not fully eliminate residual limitations. Transfer to pressure-actuated TiNi bundle is unverified. [M37-E04, M37-E06, M37-E08–E09]

## I. Relevance to Chapter 1–2 and RQs
Nguồn hỗ trợ C2.4.1 về phase/state variables và C2.4.3 về SMA-controlled friction actuator precedent. Không biến σA/EA2 thành bending stiffness hoặc friction coefficient.
