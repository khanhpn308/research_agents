# M34 — Paper Note

## A. Bibliographic information
Kotb, Elgamal, and Serry (2021), *Shape Memory Alloy Capsule Micropump for Drug Delivery Applications*. DOI: 10.3390/mi12050520. Canonical PDF: `data/chap1-2_citations/M34__2021-Shape Memory Alloy Capsule Micropump for Drug Delivery Applications.pdf`.

## B. Research problem
Thiết kế micropump capsule dùng NiTi SMA coil và polymer enclosure cho drug delivery, với reservoir thay thế và check valve tích hợp. [M34-E01]

## C. Physical mechanism and system
Joule heating chuyển SMA coil sang austenite, coil co và nén silicone enclosure; khi nguội, silicone phục hồi coil. Valve bicuspid preloaded điều chỉnh chiều dòng. [M34-E02–E03]

## D. Mechanical models and assumptions
Model endpoint giả định 100% austenite khi nóng và 100% martensite khi nguội, bỏ qua fluid effects ở trạng thái stationary. Table 1 và Eqs. (1)–(3) mô tả phase fraction, spring force và displacement compatibility. [M34-E04–E05]

## E. Experimental methods
Pump characterization đo displacement, discharge, flow rate, static head và force–displacement bằng sensor resolution 0.03 N và 0.0078 mm. [M34-E07]

## F. Important equations and figures
Fig. 1 force–deflection/phase; Fig. 2 valve; Table 1; Eqs. (1)–(3); Fig. 3 parametric map; Fig. 4 fabrication; Figs. 5–12 characterization/control; Table 3 comparison.

## G. Quantitative findings and validation
Abstract reports stroke 5.6 mm, 11 mm/s, 14 kPa at 7.1 W and 2524 µL/min; controlled 3-turn operation used 43 mm³ target volume and 790 mA with approximately 6.5 kPa static head per stroke. [M34-E01, M34-E08–E09]

## H. Limitations and counterevidence
Model is endpoint/parametric and not a full transient constitutive/contact model; sensor resolution is not complete uncertainty; pressure is pump output/static head, not local wire-bundle traction. [M34-E04, M34-E07, M34-E09]
