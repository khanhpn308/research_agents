# WP-D terminology notes

- **SMA phase/state:** martensite/austenite and R-phase are material state variables; do not equate them with friction state.
- **Tightening/clamping force:** M31 uses SMA spring geometry to change normal force at a rubber–wire interface.
- **Jamming pressure:** M32/M37 report membrane vacuum or air evacuation; this is not automatically specimen contact traction.
- **Pressure transfer:** M33/M34 concern fluidic or capsule output pressure; distinguish source pressure, chamber pressure and local interface traction.
- **Structural stiffness:** force–displacement slope or flexural rigidity of a robot/link/pump; not automatically TiNi constitutive modulus.
- **Recovery velocity:** displacement–time slope of SMA wire/device; distinct from bending stiffness, friction coefficient and thermal hysteresis.
- **σA and EA2:** M37 descriptors from tensile stress–strain curves for recovery; not global bundle bending parameters.
