# WP-C terminology notes

- **Wire rope/cable:** retain the source topology; do not relabel as layer jamming.
- **Interwire slip:** relative wire motion in helical/braided ropes; it is not automatically full slip, plasticity, or vacuum-jamming interface slip.
- **Source pressure vs contact pressure:** M35 reports interlayer radial pressure inferred from push-out tests; no vacuum source pressure is established.
- **Perfect stick / partial stick / full slip:** M24 model states, not universal physical labels for every cable.
- **Superelasticity / SME:** M25/M27–M30 refer to NiTi phase transformation; this is distinct from frictional hysteresis.
- **Hysteresis:** M24/M35/M36 contain bending/dynamic hysteresis; identify whether the source attributes it to friction, phase transformation, or phenomenological fit.
- **Validation:** distinguish primary experiment (M24, M27, M28 steel, M30, M36), literature-based comparison (M25, M29, M35) and fitted/calibrated response.
