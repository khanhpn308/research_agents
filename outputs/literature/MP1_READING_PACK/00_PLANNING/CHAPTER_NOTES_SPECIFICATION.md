# Chapter Notes specification (P2–P4 output design)

Each section note lives under `outputs/literature/MP1_READING_PACK/01_NOTES/<section_id>/` and links every record to `paper_id`, `SOURCE_REGISTRY.json`, PDF SHA-256 and physical `pdf_page`. P1 defines the structure only; no section content is populated here.

Every Section Note must use these headings in order:

A. Section objective

B. Concept and terminology notes

C. Evidence with precise source locators

D. Important equations and figures

E. Experimental and quantitative findings

F. Cross-paper comparisons

G. Limitations and counterevidence

H. Relevance to MP1

I. Reading route

J. Questions for human writing

K. Unresolved evidence

Minimum record controls:
- distinguish `AUTHOR_RESULT`, `AUTHOR_INTERPRETATION`, `AGENT_INFERENCE`, `COUNTEREVIDENCE`, and `NOT_VERIFIED`;
- use `NOT_REPORTED` when the source was checked but does not report a field;
- use `NOT_APPLICABLE` when a field does not apply;
- use `NOT_VERIFIED` when P2 has not checked it;
- block all M23 content until `P0_M23_REMEDIATION` is closed;
- do not treat a section as ready for human writing until scope, source locators, counterevidence and unresolved items are visible.
